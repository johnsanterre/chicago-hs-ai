3 · Read & play

Statements you can check

The notes list is evidence, but evidence is not yet a report. The next stage compresses notes into claims — and the word has a precise meaning here. A claim is a statement that is falsifiable: specific enough that a source could prove it wrong.

"The garden has 60 plots" is a claim — the census either says 60 or it doesn't. "The garden grew from 48 to 60 plots between 2023 and 2026" is a better claim — two numbers, two dates, checkable at both ends. "The garden is thriving" is not a claim. No source can contradict it, because it doesn't pin anything down. Feel-good sentences like that are where sloppy research hides; a research agent's report is built only from the checkable kind.

The claim record. Each claim keeps three things: the statement, the source it came from, and the exact quote that backs it — {"claim": "60 plots in 2026", "source": "cityparks.gov/report-2026", "quote": "Riverside Community Garden: 60 plots"}. The quote matters: it is the difference between "the source supports this" and "I remember the source supporting this." If you took Document Pipelines, this is the schema-prompt pattern again — same JSON contract, same null rule for anything the source doesn't actually state — now aimed at sentences instead of form fields.

Why the model does this stage. Turning "we're up to sixty plots now, from just 48 when we started the expansion push in 2023!" into a dated, two-number claim is reading for meaning. The model is good at it — and it can also invent, quietly, which is why every claim in the table goes to lesson 6 before anyone trusts it.

6 · Build

The quote audit

Take the notebook's claim table. For every claim, compare the statement to its quote and mark it: backed (the quote says exactly this), stretched (the quote says less than the claim), or unbacked (the quote doesn't support it at all). There is at least one stretch in the table.

Turn-in: the marked table and one sentence on the stretch you found — what the claim added that the quote never said.
