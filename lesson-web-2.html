3 · Read

Structure, not decoration

HTML is structure: what things ARE, not how they look. <h1> says "this is the main heading." <p> says "paragraph." <a href="…"> says "this text goes somewhere" — the link is the invention that turned documents into a web. <img src="…"> embeds a picture; <ul> with <li> items makes a list. Looks come next lesson; structure comes first.

Tags nest: a list item lives inside a list, everything lives inside <body>. Indenting your file to show that parent–child shape is the same courtesy Python's indents forced on you — here it's for the reader, and the reader is future-you.

A full page has a skeleton — <!DOCTYPE html>, a <head> for information about the page (its title, its character set), and a <body> for what's visible. Have your AI generate the skeleton once, make it explain every line, and then it's yours forever.
