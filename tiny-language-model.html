Course 7 · See inside — by building one

Build a Tiny Language Model

Course 1 told you a language model places bets on what comes next. This course builds one: counting, dice, temperature, context, real learning with a falling loss, a tokenizer — ending with a model trained on a corpus you curated, speaking a shadow of your own voice.

No other course required. The notebooks are run-the-cells friendly — if you've never opened Colab, lesson 1 of Python in Colab is a twenty-minute on-ramp, and that's all you need. Pace: one lesson a week finishes in two months; one every two weeks still finishes in a semester.
