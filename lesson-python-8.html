3 · Read

The method is the lesson

The chore bot matters less than the method you use to build it — the same method every later course assumes:

Plan in comments first. Four to six plain-language lines describing what the program will do, before any code. The plan is the design, and the design stays yours — the AI helps best when it's filling in your outline, not inventing one for you.

Build in pieces. One function at a time; run and test each before starting the next. When something breaks, you know it's in the piece you just touched — this is nine-tenths of debugging.

Sign for every line. The intern rule from day one, now with teeth: if you can't explain a line, make the AI explain it, then simplify until you can. You're about to show this work to a teacher; someday it'll be a nonprofit client. Same rule both places.

It runs twice. Top to bottom, no errors, two times in a row — the difference between "worked once on my machine" and works.
