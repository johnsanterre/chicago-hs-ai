3 · Read & play

The honesty kit

A chart is a claim about data, made visual. counts.plot(kind='barh') builds the bars; the honesty kit makes them trustworthy:

The claim title. Not "Sign-ups" but "Pilsen leads sign-ups." If the title states the point, a stranger reads the chart in three seconds — the standard your charts are graded against.

Labeled axes. Numbers without units are rumors.

Bars start at zero. A bar encodes its amount by its length; starting the axis at 70 makes a 71 and a 74 look like a 4× difference. For line charts the rule softens (small changes over time can deserve a zoom), but the default stays zero and departures get a reason.

Choosing the form: comparing categories → bars. Change over time → lines. Fancier charts exist; these two, done honestly, cover most of what a school, a team, or a nonprofit will ever ask of you.
