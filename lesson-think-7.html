3 · Read & play

Prompts with slots

Look at your last month of AI use and patterns jump out: you keep asking for the same kinds of help. An email that needs to sound right. A long thing you need the short version of. Practice questions before a test. Those repeats are worth more than any clever one-off prompt, because a repeat can become a template: a prompt you wrote carefully once, with slots for the parts that change.

Everything outside the slots is your accumulated judgment — the tone you want, the format that works, the limits that keep it honest (lesson 4 lives inside your templates now). Everything inside the slots is thirty seconds of filling in today's details.

Keep them somewhere boring and permanent: a note on your phone titled "my prompts." Five great templates you actually reuse beat fifty screenshots of clever prompts you'll never find again.

And templates are drafts too, forever: each time one comes back with something missing, add the fix to the template. Yours get better every week — that compounding is the whole reason to write them down.
