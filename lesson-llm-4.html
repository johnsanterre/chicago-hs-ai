3 · Read

Stuff, then trust — carefully

The technique is honest about itself: context stuffing — read the file, put its text in the prompt, ask the question after it. The model answers from what's in front of it, which is exactly the context window doing its job (the desk from How AI Works, lesson 2).

Two rules turn the trick into something trustworthy. Only from the document: instruct it to answer solely from the provided text — this fences out plausible-sounding inventions about your specific rules. Say when it's missing: instruct it to declare when the document doesn't contain the answer — and then test that with a question you know isn't in there. A document tool that invents policy is worse than no tool; these two lines of prompt are the whole difference.

When documents outgrow the desk, products search for the relevant chunk first and stuff only that — retrieval, the R in RAG. You've now built the core of it; the searching machinery comes later in your journey, but the concept is no longer magic.
