About · a Free Focus program

How this started

Chicago HS AI began with a chance meeting. John Santerre, founder of the nonprofit Free Focus, met Montrell Jones — a Chicago student interested in AI, computer science, and cybersecurity, and set on working in his community. They started thinking about what they could do together.

Free Focus provides technology services to nonprofits. Montrell's energy pointed the same direction, and the conversation turned to how his enthusiasm could reach the local nonprofits that need it. This program is the answer: teach Chicago high-schoolers to build with AI, then put those skills to work for organizations in their own city.

What this is

Five free courses, flat by design — no course requires another. A student learns what AI actually is, how to code with an AI partner, Python, data analysis, and how to build with language models directly. Every lesson ends with something built, and every course ends with a project in the student's own portfolio.

Where it leads

The courses are the first half. The second half is service: students who finish the toolkit take on real projects for Chicago nonprofits — the data cleaned, the website shipped, the AI helper built, for organizations doing the work in their neighborhoods. Skills learned here are meant to leave the classroom.

Free Focus

Free Focus is a nonprofit that provides technology services to nonprofits. Chicago HS AI is a Free Focus program: the same mission, carried by the next generation of builders.
