Course 10 · Answers from your own pile

Ask Your Documents

The model has never read your handbook, your club's minutes, or your notes. This course builds the system that fixes that: chunk the pile, search it by meaning with embeddings, retrieve the right pieces, and ask under a contract that keeps every answer cited, grounded, and honest about the pile's edges.

No other course required, though Build with LLMs is the gentlest on-ramp to the API pieces. Chunking, word search, and embeddings all run free in Colab with no key; the answering lessons use the class API key from the Build with LLMs setup and include precomputed outputs. Where Document Pipelines turns a pile into a table, this course answers questions from the pile's own text. Pace: one lesson a week finishes in two months; every two weeks still finishes in a semester.
