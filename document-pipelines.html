Course 8 · Turn paper piles into answers

Document Pipelines

Every club, church, and nonprofit in Chicago has the drawer: reports, forms, minutes, in every format. This course builds the assembly line that turns the drawer into a clean table and answered questions — extraction, cleaning, rules, LLM extraction, validation, storage — ending with a real pile processed end to end.

No other course required. The notebooks are run-the-cells friendly — if Colab is new to you, lesson 1 of Python in Colab is a twenty-minute on-ramp. Lessons 5 and 7 use the class API key from the Build with LLMs setup; both include precomputed outputs so they work as reading without one. This is the most practicum-shaped course on the site — document piles are what nonprofits actually have. Pace: one lesson a week finishes in two months; every two weeks still finishes in a semester.
